License texts and source links fetched from upstream repositories are collected in `LICENSES/`; the source snapshots have been checked for their listed binary versions where possible. Read the upstream notices before modifying or redistributing any bundled component. This repository does not claim authorship of the upstream software or unidentified ACPI binaries.
